# Monetization — Original Content Rewards reference

This reference summarizes the program assumptions used by the coach. X can change eligibility, payout, and measurement rules; the Premium dashboard and current X documentation are authoritative. Ranking signals and payout eligibility are related but should not be treated as a guarantee of either reach or revenue.

## What a qualified impression is

Unique **Home Timeline** impression from a **Premium/verified** user, with **at least half the post visible**, excluding paid and fraudulent views. Practical reading:

- Premium users are mostly people who don't follow you yet → **out-of-network reach is the revenue lever**, which is exactly what the For You score already optimizes.
- "Half the post visible" favors posts that hold the scroll — same dwell game as ranking.
- **Replies earn nothing** (since early 2026). An idea worth money goes in a standalone post; the reply is where the link lives.

## What counts as original

Your own voice, perspective, expertise, reporting, or creativity: original writing, analysis, photos, videos, memes, and commentary that **adds meaningful perspective**. A QT with a real take qualifies; a bare repost doesn't. Explicitly not enough: copies, reuploads, automated content, "caption, crop, border, watermark, speed adjustment, or simple text overlay".

Clickbait and engagement farming are penalized on the payout side too (announced Apr 2026) — the same behaviors the ranking already punishes via the click-dwell/low-fav penalty and dwell-regret gates. There is no tension between the two systems: don't bait, ever.

## Eligibility reference

18+ · eligible country · X Premium (any tier) · 500 verified followers · **500k Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days** · posting original content regularly. These are a historical reference point only. Numbers can change; the Premium dashboard is authoritative and this file provides writing guidance.
